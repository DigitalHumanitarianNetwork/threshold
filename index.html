The problem

General benchmarks test general knowledge. They do not test yours.

AI models are being used for specialised work with no shared way to check whether they actually know the subject. A model that sounds confident can still be wrong about the things that matter in your field.

What it is

Your questions. Your standard. Their scores.

Threshold Evaluations is a service for testing AI models on the topics you know best.

Your questions

You build a set of questions on your topic, the way an expert would ask them.

Your standard

For each question you write the ideal answer: the key points a good answer must cover.

Their scores

The service asks every model your questions, checks each answer against your key points, and gives each model a score and a report.

How it works

Three steps, run as often as you need.

01

Ask

Your question set is sent to the models you choose, whenever you want: on a schedule, or when a new model comes out.

02

Score

Each answer is checked against your key points and scored from 0 to 1. A score of 0.7 or more is a pass.

03

Report

You get a per-model report: overall score, question by question, and how scores change over time.

Public good, private tests

The test stays private. The results are public.

Your questions and ideal answers stay private, so models cannot learn the test. Only aggregate scores are published, so everyone can see how models measure up over time.
