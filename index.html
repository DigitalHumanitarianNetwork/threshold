Threshold

The minimum standard AI models should meet before they are trusted with humanitarian work.

Work in progress. This is a placeholder page. The project has only just begun, and nothing here is a finished service or result yet.

The idea

More and more people in the humanitarian sector are turning to AI models for answers. Threshold is a planned evaluation service that tests how well those models do on real humanitarian questions, so people can see where they are reliable and where they are not.

Real questions

Questions drawn from the kinds of things humanitarian practitioners actually need to know, not generic quiz material.

Reference answers

Each question comes with an ideal answer, written and reviewed by people who know the work.

Public good, private tests

Results are meant to be shared openly. The test questions and answer keys are kept private so models cannot simply learn them.

Where things stand

We are starting with the humanitarian sector, learning what people need, and defining how questions, scoring and answer keys should work. If it goes well, the approach could later be adapted to other fields. The details, including the first question sets and any results, will be published here as they are ready.
