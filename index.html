Just getting started

Threshold

The minimum standard AI models should meet before they are trusted with humanitarian work.

Work in progress. This is a placeholder page. The project has only just begun, and nothing here is a finished service or result yet.

The idea

People working in humanitarian response are increasingly turning to AI for answers. Threshold is a planned evaluation service to test model performance against real questions from this work, helping people understand what they can rely on and where the limits are.

Real questions

Questions based on what humanitarian practitioners need to know in their work.

Reference answers

Each question will have an ideal answer prepared and reviewed by people with relevant experience.

Public good, private tests

Results are intended to be shared openly, while test questions and answer keys stay private so models cannot simply learn them.

Where things stand

The work is starting with humanitarian use cases: learning what practitioners need and working out how questions, scoring and answer keys should be handled. If it proves useful, the approach could later be adapted to other fields. Details and results will be added here when they are ready.
